HELL FIGHT

THE WARDEN has no mercy and no healing. Attacks are random, they stack, and they get faster. You have 48 HP and every hit costs 4. Survive 90 seconds. Arrow keys / WASD or drag to move. Shift to dash (1 second cooldown), and you cannot be hit while dashing. Sometimes the arena stretches wider or taller. That does not count toward the attack limit.

EASY: up to 4 attacks at once · NORMAL: up to 6 · HELL: up to 9
